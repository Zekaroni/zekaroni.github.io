Core Philosophy

Red-Eyes in Duel Links is no longer a simple beatdown deck. It thrives on Graveyard Management and Equip Synergy. The objective is to utilize the "Red-Eyes Armament" skill to consistently fetch "Red-Eyes Baby Dragon" or "Black Metal Dragon" while maintaining a disruptive backrow.

The Fusion Turbo

The aggressive play focuses on resolving Red-Eyes Fusion. This allows the player to send materials directly from the Deck to the Graveyard, thinning the deck while summoning a 3000+ ATK boss monster immediately.
